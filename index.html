Before we use your camera

  • Your face is processed on this device to place make-up on it.
  • No picture or video is uploaded or kept.
  • You can stop at any time with the close button or by saying "stop".
Read the full notice

This prototype uses an on-device face model to find 478 points on your face. Points are used only while the try-on is open and are discarded when you close it. A real service would also explain how long any data is kept, who can see it and how to delete it, in this same plain language.

Keyboard: press A to agree, Escape to go back. Voice: say "I agree".
